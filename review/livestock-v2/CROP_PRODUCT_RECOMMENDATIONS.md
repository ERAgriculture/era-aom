# Crop-product hierarchy recommendations

Decision record for 32 missing-parent cases spanning crop products and four product
branches misplaced beneath Crop Byproduct.

## Approved disposition

- Mint 24 governed concepts using `AOM_100921`–`AOM_100944`: Crop product; missing
  cash-crop, spice, and vegetable product categories; and useful source-product groups.
- Apply eight reparenting decisions where reviewed category/source concepts already exist
  or legacy placement is demonstrably wrong.
- Keep primary crop products separate from production and processing by-products.
- Reuse existing Cereal Products, Fruit Product, Legume Products, Oil Plant Products,
  Roots and Tuber Products, Wheat, Date, and Noug contexts rather than mint duplicates.

## Corrections and exceptions

- `AOM_001818` Ensete ventricosum Corm was filed beneath “Sweet potato tubers, fresh |
  Feedipedia.” Scientific name and label agree on Ensete; it moves to Ensete products.
- Date, Wheat Grain, and Cotton Seed move out of Crop Byproduct product subpaths.
- Existing singular `AOM_000665` Fruit Product absorbs children from missing plural
  “Fruit Products”; no spelling-only duplicate is minted.
- Black cumin seed cakes receive a by-product grouping, separate from Black cumin seed
  and ground product forms.

## Deferred classification

Seven mixed-boundary records—residue, cake, pulp, sludge, and similar materials—remain
navigable but are queued for explicit product/by-product review. Batch does not hide
uncertainty by silently asserting precise economic-product status.
