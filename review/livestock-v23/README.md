# Final definition-tail closure

Single final cohort covering all 55 definition gaps remaining after ontology-
integrity review. Thirty-seven authority-repair, named commercial-product, and
local-term concepts receive bounded operational definitions. Eighteen identity
or semantic-model cases remain explicit holds.

Commercial definitions do not assert manufacturer, formulation, composition,
or efficacy. Local-term definitions do not assert biological identity or
synonymy. Warned, unreachable, broad, or contradictory external mappings never
become definition evidence. Held concepts remain visible and actionable.
