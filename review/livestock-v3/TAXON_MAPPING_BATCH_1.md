# Taxon mapping review — batch 1

Status: proposed for review. No mapping is approved by this file.

Batch covers ten high-impact source taxa without private counts or records.
NCBI identifiers, current names, and ranks were checked against live NCBI
Taxonomy records on 2026-08-04. WFO legacy candidates remain held pending
independent nomenclatural verification.

Important decisions:

- `Pennisetum purpureum` remains source evidence but NCBI current name is
  `Cenchrus purpureus`; same `NCBITaxon_154765` identifier is retained.
- `Gossypium` is genus rank and must never be inferred as one cotton species.
- `Ostreidae` is family rank and must never be inferred as one oyster species.
- One reviewed taxon may classify many feed-material concepts; material and
  organism identities remain separate.

Approval requires reviewer/date plus confirmation that each linked AOM feed
material genuinely derives from stated organism. Name-level verification alone
does not prove every material-to-taxon assertion.
