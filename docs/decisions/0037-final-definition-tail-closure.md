# ADR 0037: Final definition-tail closure

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Resolve all 55 remaining definition cases in one final governance cohort.
Approve 37 bounded operational definitions for authority-repair, named
commercial-product, and local-term records. Retain 18 explicit identity and
semantic-model holds.

Canonical AOM identity can define an operational record without asserting facts
not present in the workbook. Commercial definitions omit manufacturer,
formulation, composition, and efficacy. Local definitions omit biological
identity and synonymy. Warned, unavailable, broad, or contradictory mappings
remain non-definitional. Identity merges and missing facets remain blocked until
their evidence gates are met.
