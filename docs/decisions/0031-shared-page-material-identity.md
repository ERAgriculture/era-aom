# ADR 0031: Shared-page material identity

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Never infer synonymy from two AOM concepts sharing one Feedipedia page. Treat
page headings as definition-grade only for an explicitly named material member;
keep broader source, different component, different process, and different
species concepts distinct.

Review all 32 concepts across 16 shared pages together. Approve six explicit
materials using existing facets: pearl-millet grain, Ensete corm, palm-oil mill
residue, Opuntia cladode, cocoa-bean cake, and linseed cake. Retain 26 holds.
Potential Faidherbia/Acacia, Gliricidia spelling, and Centrosema synonym pairs
require occurrence evidence before deprecation or replacement. ILRI feed codes
remain out of scope.
