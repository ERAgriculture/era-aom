# ADR 0032: Workbook-only product evidence

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Use canonical workbook hierarchy as definition evidence only when it establishes
a stable generic material role that can be represented with approved facets.
Do not infer branded-product composition, manufacturer, biological identity, or
geographic meaning from a label and parent path.

Review complete 23-concept cohort together. Approve four bounded materials:
Lignobond and ChemBind as named binders with unspecified composition, mixed
marine hydrolysate as mixed hydrolysed animal by-product, and Cheka residue as a
named cereal-processing residue. Retain 19 explicit holds. Correct
`Lactobacillus plantarum` hierarchy before defining it; current prebiotic parent
is semantically wrong. ILRI feed codes remain out of scope.
