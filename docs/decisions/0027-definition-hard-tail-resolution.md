# ADR 0027: Definition hard-tail resolution

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Review final 210 definition gaps as one evidence cohort. Approve core and feed
category scope directly from governed hierarchy plus cited category evidence.
For derived materials, require governed source identity and complete explicit
descriptor coverage using existing approved component, process, product-role,
form, or constituent facets.

Do not use lexical similarity alone. Do not treat oil as physical form. Do not
map rhizome to root or approve compound identity when an explicit component is
unmodelled. Shared, warned, unreachable, conflicting, commercial, or locally
ambiguous evidence remains held with blocker and next action. Definitions remain
generated from structured decisions; ingestion never reparses definition text.
