# ADR 0036: Ontology-integrity tail

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Review all 32 semantic-model and identity-integrity cases together. Approve 14
bounded operational material identities where canonical AOM scope remains valid
after contradictory external mappings are excluded. Keep 18 explicit holds for
replacement direction, corrected identity, hierarchy, and missing facets.

Never infer stem from shaft, vine from vein, or synonym replacement from lexical
similarity alone. Preserving a legacy identifier does not require publishing an
incorrect external mapping. Duplicate consolidation remains gated by occurrence
evidence and an explicit replacement relation.
