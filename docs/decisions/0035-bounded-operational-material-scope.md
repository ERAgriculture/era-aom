# ADR 0035: Bounded operational material scope

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Use canonical AOM workbook identity and hierarchy to define an operational feed
material at source scope when external mappings are broader, narrower, or
taxonomic. Explicitly state that component, whole-material integrity, process,
form, composition, and nutritional properties are unspecified unless separate
approved facets exist.

This closes all 40 material-scope definition holds without treating a taxon as
a whole plant, copying external definitions, or turning related mappings into
identity evidence. Existing explicit descriptors may add only already governed
facets. Raw labels, mappings, and provenance remain preserved.
