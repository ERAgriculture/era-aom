# ADR 0033: Consolidated authority and model review

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Review related evidence routes as one 63-concept governance batch. Approve only
materials whose biological or generic source identity is established and whose
remaining label semantics map to existing explicit facets. Cross-domain public
mappings remain discovery evidence, not identity shortcuts.

Approve 17 bounded materials covering cakes, oils, anatomical parts, discards,
residues, pollard, molasses, and oyster shells. Retain 46 evidence-specific
holds. Never approximate cassava shaft as stem, plant veins as vines, or African
palm larvae from plant taxonomy. Do not publish branded-product composition
without stable manufacturer evidence. ILRI feed codes remain out of scope.
