# ADR 0026: Canonical-workbook source-scope cohort

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Review all 200 source-workbook-routed definition gaps as one frozen cohort.
Canonical workbook may establish controlled identity, hierarchy placement, and
category role. It does not by itself establish chemistry, nutrition, component,
process, physical form, or equivalence to an external vocabulary.

Approve bounded identity definitions for unambiguous workbook terms and bounded
classification definitions for structural categories. Hold commercial names,
locally ambiguous labels, contradictory categories, and unmodelled compound
component/process terms pending citable evidence and structured assertions. Preserve two
workbook-versus-normalized path differences for audit; make no hierarchy change
through definition review. Ingestion consumes structured assertions and never
reparses definition or preferred-label text.
