# ADR 0030: Identity and alias evidence boundary

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-06
- Owner: Alliance of Bioversity International and CIAT
- Reviewer: Pete Steward

## Decision

Treat source-page co-reference as related evidence, not synonymy. Approve a
Feedipedia identity only when current heading or parent datasheet establishes
same material independent of word order, accepted common name, spelling
variant, or narrower table processing state. Compose definitions from governed
source identity and existing explicit facets; do not copy nutritional prose.

Review complete frozen 39-concept cohort together. Approve nine exact or
bounded identities and retain 30 explicit holds. Correct `Ziziphus mauritiania`
to `Ziziphus mauritiana` and `Cynadon dactylon` to `Cynodon dactylon`, while
keeping both material scopes held. Previously removed contradictory mappings
remain excluded. ILRI feed-code evidence remains out of scope.
